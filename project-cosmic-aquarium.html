The sky and the sea, two vast unexplored spaces, in one installation.

A lighting installation about the similarities between the sky and the sea, especially how much of both is still unexplored. It was made from recycled plastic collected in our office.

My role

I met weekly with the design-build team for several months before the installation, developing concepts, sketches and the construction process.
